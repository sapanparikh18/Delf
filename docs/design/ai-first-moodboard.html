Recommendation for AI-First

Option A (Paris Sunshine) remains the strongest all-around choice for the widest age range and competitive differentiation. Option B (Jardin) is the runner-up if longer AI conversation sessions are the priority — the calmer palette reduces visual fatigue during extended voice practice. Option D (Les Bonbons) is ideal if the target skews 11–18 and the brand wants a more modern, premium feel.
