# Color Palette Options — Le Petit Francais

Inspired by Sanzo Wada's *A Dictionary of Color Combinations* (1933).
Each palette pulls from specific Wada combinations, extended with
neutral background/surface/text colors for a complete app system.

---

## Recommendation

**Option A ("Paris Sunshine")** is the strongest all-around choice:
- Broadest age appeal (8-14)
- Excellent gender neutrality
- High energy without being overwhelming
- Strong Wada heritage (Combinations #257 + #250)
- The 4 colors map naturally to the 4 DELF worlds
- Orange Yellow as primary = optimism + learning
- The palette is distinctive vs. competitors (Duolingo = green, Babbel = orange)

**Runner-up: Option B ("Jardin de Rémi")** if we want a calmer, more
focus-oriented feel — better for longer study sessions.
